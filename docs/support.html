What the app does

License Plate Bingo Road Trip is a road-trip game for spotting license plates and marking bingo cards. Modes include USA, East Coast, Kid Colors, and Road Watch.

Common questions

  • Does the app need an account? No.
  • Does it use location or the camera? No, not in the current version.
  • In-app purchases? None. The App Store price is a paid download only.
