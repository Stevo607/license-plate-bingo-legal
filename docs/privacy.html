Privacy Policy

License Plate Bingo Road Trip

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the License Plate Bingo Road Trip iOS app (“the App”), published by Stephen Paolicelli, handles information.

Summary

The App does not collect personal data. Gameplay runs on your device. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, in-app purchases inside the App, GPS/location features, or camera features in the current version.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal information through the App. Scores and game progress stay on your device unless Apple provides optional system features you control separately.

Third parties

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. App Store purchase and distribution are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

Children

The App is designed as a family-friendly road-trip game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: paolicelli@gmail.com

Changes

If the App later adds features that collect data, this page will be updated before those features ship, and App Store privacy labels will be updated to match.
